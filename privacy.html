Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

YuMadeIt (yumadeit.org) is a personal website run by Hyeonho Henry Yu, Ph.D., showcasing free web tools built for teachers. This page explains what information is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Information we collect

YuMadeIt does not require an account and does not ask you to enter personal information on this site. If you submit a tool request, you do so through a Google Form, which is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Information you share in the form is used only to respond to your request.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Linked tools

The gallery links to separate tools and sites. Those sites may have their own privacy practices, and this policy covers only yumadeit.org.

Children's privacy

YuMadeIt is intended for teachers and other adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the request form.
