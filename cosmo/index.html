Cosmo's Planet Expedition

An interactive solar system adventure

Pilot the starship Cosmo across the solar system and land on each planet to observe it up close. Fly near a planet and you'll touch down automatically, opening an observation card.

←↑↓→ or WASD to fly. On mobile, use the on-screen arrow buttons.

The Sun is far too hot to land on. Keep your distance!
